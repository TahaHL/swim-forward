1. WHAT THIS IS
- Owner: Taha, a qualified Level 2 swimming teacher in West London (sole trader).
- Swim Progress Plan is supplementary swimming coaching that runs alongside a child's regular lessons. Small groups (up to four, 30-minute sessions) or 1:1, usually as a six-week development programme.
- The app lets the coach record technique assessments quickly at the poolside and lets parents see, for their own child only:
  - how the last session went
  - what improved
  - what needs work and why
  - the one next coaching focus
  - the next session
- Separate technique ratings sit alongside, and never replace, any provider's stage outcomes.
- Version 1 serves the owner's own coaching. Design the data model so more coaches or a second business could be added later, but do not build multi-business features now.
- Tagline: "Every Skill. Every Milestone. Every Step Forward."

3. ASSESSMENT RULES (do not change)
- Five labels in fixed order: Not Assessed, Needs Practice, Fair, Good, Pass.
- Only Pass completes a skill. Good never counts as Pass.
- Not Assessed means no current assessment and is excluded from figures.
- Every assessment is kept in history. A downgrade or reset changes the current state but deletes nothing.
- A Pass is for one skill and is never described as a stage award.
- No numeric scores, averages, percentages-as-grades or predictions.
- Label descriptions stay marked provisional until I confirm them in the app.

5. VERSION 1 SCOPE
Roles: owner (coach), parent. Design for an additional coach role later.

Coach:
- Create swimmers with minimal data: first name, last name, year of birth, current lesson stage (free text), and up to two parent emails.
- Groups, six-week programmes and a session schedule with venue, plus attendance marking.
- A fast poolside assessment sheet:
  - five buttons per skill
  - optional feedback and next target per skill
  - one session note and one next coaching focus
  - a single "Publish update" action
  - drafts kept safely until published
  - works on a phone in wet hands: large targets, minimal typing
- A skill library editor to add, edit and retire skills and criteria per stroke.
  - Front crawl ships with the 17 existing skills.
  - Other strokes stay hidden until I add and approve their criteria.
- Invite parents by email: single-use links that expire after 7 days, with revocable access.
- Preview exactly what a parent will see.
- Export or permanently delete a swimmer's records.
- Audit log of every assessment change: who, when, old and new value.

Parent:
- Sign in with an emailed code.
- First sign-in captures consent to data use, with the privacy notice linked and a version recorded.
- Home: how it went, what improved, what needs work and why, one next focus, next session. No large percentage figure.
- Progress:
  - per-skill checklist
  - skill detail with rating, feedback, next target and history
  - a "Then and now" comparison between any two weeks
- Achievements: one per skill reaching Pass.
- Email alert "You have a new update from your coach". The email contains no assessment content, only a sign-in link.
- Download their child's records. Request deletion.
- Read-only: parents can never change assessments and only ever see their own child.

6. SECURITY AND PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- Row-level security tests that prove:
  - a parent cannot read another child's data
  - a parent cannot write anything
  - a coach can only reach their own swimmers
- Secrets only in environment variables. Nothing sensitive in logs, URLs, analytics or error reports.
- Rate limiting on sign-in and invitations. Origin and input validation on every write.
- Retention: a configurable period after a programme ends, with automatic deletion and a dry-run report first.
- Daily backups, plus a tested restore procedure written up in RUNBOOK.md.
- Privacy notice, terms and consent wording as clearly marked TEMPLATES for my solicitor to review. Do not present them as final or legally checked.
- Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard, focus, contrast, labels and 390 px mobile, with an automated scan in CI.
