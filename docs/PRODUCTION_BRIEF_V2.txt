==================================================
PRODUCT VISION
==================================================

Swim Progress Plan is an ongoing supplementary swimming coaching programme.

Parents book coaching in six-week blocks, typically corresponding to approximately half a term.

It operates ALONGSIDE a child's normal swimming lessons rather than replacing them.

The fundamental problem we are solving is this:

A parent may see that their child remains at Fair or Good, or stays in the same swimming stage for a long time, but not understand WHY.

For example, a broad front-crawl kicking outcome may hide several distinct technical issues.

Swim Progress Plan breaks technique into understandable components such as:

- body alignment
- head/body position
- alternating flutter kick
- kick generated from the hips
- relaxed ankles
- consistent kicking rhythm
- arm action
- hand entry
- breathing timing
- body position while breathing
- arm/leg coordination
- maintaining technique over distance

The exact teaching framework must remain editable by authorised staff.

This product does NOT award official Swim England stages.

It provides supplementary coaching observations and development targets.

==================================================
THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

A parent should be able to open the app for 30–60 seconds after their child's lesson and immediately answer:

1. What is my child doing well?
2. What are they struggling with?
3. Why are they struggling with it?
4. What is the teacher working on next?
5. Has this improved compared with previous weeks?

If the parent has to interpret complex charts, navigate six screens, understand swimming terminology or calculate anything themselves, the UX has failed.

DESIGN FOR NON-TECHNICAL, BUSY PARENTS FIRST.

Progressive disclosure is essential.

Show the simple answer first.
Allow technical detail to be opened when wanted.
